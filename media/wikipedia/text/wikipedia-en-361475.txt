The hippocampus, or hippocamp (pl. hippocampi – pl. hippocamps; 
Ancient Greek: ἱππόκαμπος, romanized: hippókampos, from ἵππος, 'horse', and κάμπος, 'sea monster'), is a mythological creature mentioned in Etruscan, Greek, Phoenician, Pictish and Roman mythologies, typically depicted as having the upper body of a horse with the lower body of a fish.
