Colin from Accounts is an Australian romantic dramedy television series created and written by husband-and-wife team Patrick Brammall and Harriet Dyer, who also star as the show's main characters, Gordon and Ashley. It is produced by Easy Tiger Productions and CBS Studios for Foxtel, and streams in Australia on Binge. The show aired on BBC Two in the UK and streams on BBC iPlayer. It started streaming on Paramount+ in the US on 9 November 2023.
