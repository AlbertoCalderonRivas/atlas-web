Changeling: The Lost is the fifth supplementary role-playing game line published by White Wolf Publishing as part of the Chronicles of Darkness setting. It uses the Storytelling System for rules. Changeling is the second limited game published by White Wolf after Promethean: The Created, but in April 2008 it was announced that because of the success of the line it would be an extended limited series not limited to the five books originally planned. Since then, three full hard-cover books were published: Equinox Road, Dancers in the Dusk, and Swords at Dawn.
