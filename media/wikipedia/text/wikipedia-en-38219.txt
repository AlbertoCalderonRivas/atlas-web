Toilet humour (or scatological humor) is a type of off-colour humour dealing with: defecation, diarrhoea, constipation, urination, flatulence (see flatulence humor); or, to a lesser extent, vomiting and other bodily functions.
Toilet humour is commonly an interest of both children and adults, for whom cultural taboos related to acknowledgement of waste excretion still have a degree of novelty. The humour comes from the rejection of such taboos, and is a part of modern culture.
