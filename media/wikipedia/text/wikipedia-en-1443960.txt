Castlevania: Curse of Darkness is a 2005 action role-playing game developed and published by Konami for the PlayStation 2 and Xbox. It is the fourth 3D title in the Castlevania series, following Castlevania: Lament of Innocence (2003). The game received mixed-to-positive reviews from journalists, with praise for its combat system, mechanics, and music, though many criticized its repetitive level design.
