Giffords Circus is a traditional English circus that tours the Cotswolds and south of England every summer, also performing at Chiswick House. The circus features a wide mixture of performer types from various nations, including acrobatics, aerialist and trapeze artistes, tight-rope walkers, stunt riders, opera singers, dancers, clowns, illusionists, and more. 
Founded in 2000 by Nell Gifford and her then husband Toti Gifford, the circus has continued to tour yearly (excluding 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic) in the summer months since its beginning. Each year the circus has a different theme, with its latest edition being "Waterfield", which creates a magical rendition of the English countryside and the animals that live within it.
