Britney Jean Spears (born December 2, 1981) is an American singer. Referred to as the "Princess of Pop", she is known for her unique vocal style and energetic stage performances. Spears's role in reviving teen pop music established her as the best-selling teenage artist of all time and one of the most influential entertainers of the early 21st century.
Spears began her career in the early 1990s as a cast member of the musical Ruthless!
