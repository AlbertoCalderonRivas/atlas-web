Demonstrations and protests against poor economic and school conditions by workers and lycée (high school) students have taken place in France since September 2026. In early October, the protests developed into a nationwide mobilization involving students, teachers, parents, political organizations, the yellow vests and trade unions, with an estimated 575,000–1.57 million people participating nationwide.
Labour movements emerged in mid-September in reaction to the cost-of-living and fuel crisis caused by the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz and Bab al-Mandab Strait, spearheaded by a newly revived yellow vests movement. Causes also included high inflation, housing, economic stagnation, social inequality and unemployment, coupled with austerity measures and social spending cuts resulting from the public debt crisis.
