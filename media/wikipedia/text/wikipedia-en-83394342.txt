The Hundred-Eyed Demon Lord (Chinese: 百眼魔君; pinyin: Bǎiyǎn Mójūn), also known as the Centipede Demon (Chinese: 蜈蚣精; pinyin: Wúgōng jīng), is an antagonist from the 16th-century Chinese classic novel Journey to the West. A giant centipede in his true form, he disguises himself as a Daoist priest and is among the most powerful demons encountered by Sun Wukong during the pilgrimage.
