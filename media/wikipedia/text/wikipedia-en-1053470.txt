A public toilet, also known as a restroom, bathroom or washroom, is a room or small building with sinks and toilets (and sometimes urinals) for use by the general public. The facilities are available to customers, travelers, employees of a business, school pupils or prisoners. Public toilets are typically found in many different places: inner-city locations, offices, factories, schools, universities and other places of work and study. Similarly, museums, cinemas, bars, restaurants, and entertainment venues usually provide public toilets.
