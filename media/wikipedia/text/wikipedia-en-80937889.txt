The Royal Academy Exhibition of 1856 was the annual Summer Exhibition of the British Royal Academy of Arts, the eighty eighth to be held. It was staged at the National Gallery in London between 5 May and 26 July 1856 during the Victorian era. Taking place the year the Crimean War ended with the Treaty of Paris, a number of paintings made reference to the recent conflict. Peace Concluded by John Everett Millais features a wounded army officer reading news of the peace.
