George Walker Bush (born July 6, 1946) is an American retired politician and businessman who served as the 43rd president of the United States from 2001 to 2009. A member of the Republican Party, he previously served as the 46th governor of Texas from 1995 to 2000. He is the eldest son of George H. W. Bush, the 41st president.
Born in New Haven, Connecticut, Bush graduated from Yale University in 1968 with a bachelor's degree in history.
