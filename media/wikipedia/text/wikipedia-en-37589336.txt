The Changeling (Japanese: 取り替え子 (チェンジリング), romanized: Torikaeko (Chenjiringu)) is a 2000 novel by Kenzaburō Ōe. It is the first book of a trilogy. A translation into English by Deborah Boliver Boehm (ISBN 9780802119360), was published in 2010 by Grove Press in the United States and by Atlantic Books in the United Kingdom. Boehm uses American English heavily in her translation.
