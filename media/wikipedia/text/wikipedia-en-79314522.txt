Bathsheba (Italian: Betsabea) is an 1827 history painting by the Italian artist Francesco Hayez. It features the biblical figure of Bathsheba in a nude scene, who is watched bathing by the Israelite king David. She is shown facing away from the viewer and wearing a single bangle on her arm while David can be seen at a high vantage point at the top of the painting. Numerous paintings had been produced from the Renaissance era onwards featuring the scene.
