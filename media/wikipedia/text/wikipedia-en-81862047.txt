Bhabiji Ghar Par Hain! Fun on the Run (transl. Is Sister-in-Law at Home! Fun on the Run) is a 2026 Indian Hindi-language comedy drama film directed by Shashank Bali. It is presented by Zee Studios and produced under Edit II Productions and Zee Cinema, the film stars Aasif Sheikh, Rohitashv Gour, Shubhangi Atre, Vidisha Srivastava, Ravi Kishan, Mukesh Tiwari and Dinesh Lal Yadav.
