Kukur Tihar (Nepali: कुकुर तिहार) is an annual Hindu festival originating from Nepal. It is celebrated on the second day of the five-day Tihar festival, it is dedicated to the worship and honoring of dogs, reflecting the bond between humans and dogs. The day is observed as a public holiday in Nepal.
The festival follows the Nepal's national Bikram Sambat calendar, and takes place during the lunar month of Kartik (typically falling between October and mid-November in the Gregorian calendar).
