Clancy's Tavern is the seventeenth studio album by American country music artist Toby Keith. 
It was released on October 25, 2011, by Show Dog-Universal Music. Lead-off single "Made in America" was released on June 13, 2011, reaching number one on the country charts in October 2011. Clancy's Tavern draws its name from the title track, which Keith wrote about his grandmother's bar in Fort Smith, Arkansas.
