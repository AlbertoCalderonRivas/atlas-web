Mashimaro (Korean: 마시마로) or Yeopgi Tokki (엽기토끼; lit. 'bizarre rabbit') is a South Korean fictional character who resembles a fat rabbit, created by Kim Jae In (김재인). Mashimaro debuted in a series of Flash animations (mostly without any dialogue) on the internet. Mashimaro is far more often seen in merchandise, similar to Hello Kitty.
