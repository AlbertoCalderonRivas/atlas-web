Super Robot Monkey Team Hyperforce Go! (also known as SRMTHFG or simply Super Robot Monkey Team) is an American animated television series created by Ciro Nieli. It was produced by Walt Disney Television Animation, with animation being done by The Answer Studio in Japan, and is thus sometimes considered an anime because of it. The series was the first original show produced for the Jetix block and for the Jetix and Disney channels worldwide, where the show premiered on September 18, 2004.
