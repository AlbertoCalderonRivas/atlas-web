Nathan Joseph Fielder (born May 12, 1983) is a Canadian filmmaker, actor, pilot, and comedian. He is known for his awkward, deadpan persona and his use of satire and surreal humor.
Born and raised in Vancouver, Fielder was involved in his school's improv group as a teenager, which featured fellow comedian Seth Rogen. He majored in business at the University of Victoria, which influenced his later work.
