Morph is a fictional superhero appearing in the American animated superhero series X-Men: The Animated Series—which aired on Fox Kids from 1992 to 1997—and its revival X-Men '97, which has been streaming on Disney+ since 2024. Morph is introduced as an X-Men member who sacrificed themself to protect Wolverine from a Sentinel in the show's premiere. The second season revealed that Mister Sinister resurrected and experimented on Morph, turning them into his minion. After the X-Men free Morph from Sinister's control, they are taken to Muir Island to recuperate.
