The Changeling is a 2017 fantasy horror novel by Victor LaValle. The novel received critical acclaim, winning awards including the 2017 Dragon Award for Best Horror Novel, 2018 British Fantasy Award for Best Horror Novel (August Derleth Award), 2018 Locus Award for Best Horror Novel, and 2018 World Fantasy Award—Novel. The novel was adapted into a television show of the same name which premiered in 2023.
