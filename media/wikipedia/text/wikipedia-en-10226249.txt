A calico cat is a domestic cat of any breed with a tricolor coat of black-based and red-based pigments with white. Outside of North American English, this coat pattern is called tricolor or tortoiseshell and white.
Calico is not to be confused with a tortoiseshell, which is a bicolored coat of black-based and red-based pigments, without white markings. The calico cat is most commonly thought of as being 25% to 75% white with large orange and black patches; however, they may have other colors and patterns.
