Talking Tom & Friends(formerly known as Talking Friends) is a Slovenian video game series and multimedia franchise created and owned by Outfit7 Limited, a Slovenian video game developer. The franchise is best known for focusing on various mobile games involving anthropomorphic animal characters repeating things said by the user. The first app, Talking Tom Cat, was launched on June 26, 2010. As of June 2022, the apps have achieved more than 18 billion downloads.
