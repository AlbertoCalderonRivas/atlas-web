Puti Zushi (simplified Chinese: 菩提祖师; traditional Chinese: 菩提祖師; pinyin: Pútí Zǔshī), also called Subhuti Zushi (須菩提祖師), is a character in the 16th-century Chinese novel Journey to the West. He is the first teacher of Sun Wukong, whom he names and teaches the secrets of immortality, the seventy-two transformations, and the somersault cloud. His name comes from the Buddhist figure Subhūti, but his teachings include both Buddhist and Daoist ideas.
