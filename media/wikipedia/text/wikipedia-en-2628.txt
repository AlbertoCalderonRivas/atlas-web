The aegis or egis ( EE-jis; Ancient Greek: αἰγίς aigís), as stated in the Iliad, is a device carried by Zeus, Apollo and Athena,  variously interpreted as an animal skin or a shield and sometimes featuring the head of a Gorgon. It may also be represented as a breastplate, primarily by Roman authors. There may be a connection with a deity named Aex, a daughter of Helios and a nurse of Zeus or alternatively a mistress of Zeus (Hyginus, Astronomica 2. 13).
