Cats in the Museum (Russian: Коты Эрмитажа, romanized: Koty Ermitazha, lit. 'Cats of the Hermitage') is a 2023 Russian animated comedy film directed, written, produced, and edited by Vasiliy Rovenskiy. The film follows Vincent, a ginger cat, and Maurice, a mouse, team up with other cats to protect pieces of artwork in the Hermitage Museum.
The film was released in Russia on March 23, 2023.
A sequel Cats in the Museum 2: Treasures of Egypt was released on March 26, 2026.
