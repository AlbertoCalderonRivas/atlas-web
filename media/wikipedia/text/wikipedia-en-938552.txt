The Working Families Party (WFP) is a progressive minor political party in the United States. WFP promotes for economic progressivism, left-wing populism, and cultural liberalism.
WFP was organized in 1998 as a successor to the New Party by a coalition of labor unions including CWA and SEIU, advocacy groups including Citizen Action, and community organizations including ACORN. Founded in New York, WFP has active chapters in nearly two dozen states. WFP has statewide ballot access in Connecticut, New York, and Oregon.
