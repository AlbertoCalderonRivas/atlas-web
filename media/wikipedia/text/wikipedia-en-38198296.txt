The White Dragon Horse, known in Chinese as Bai Longma (Chinese: 白龍馬; pinyin: Bái Lóng Mǎ; Wade–Giles: Pai² Lung²-ma¹; lit. 'White Dragon Horse') and Yulong (Chinese: 玉龍; pinyin: Yù Lóng; Wade–Giles: Yü⁴ Lung²; lit. 'Jade Dragon'), is a major character in the 16th-century Chinese novel Journey to the West.
